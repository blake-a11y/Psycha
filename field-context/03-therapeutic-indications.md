# 03 · Therapeutic indications under study

**Phase:** 1 · **Scope:** in-scope · **Format:** Markdown with file paths

## What it is

The set of clinical conditions for which psychedelic-assisted therapy is in active trial development.

## Primary indications

### Treatment-resistant depression (TRD)
- US adults with TRD: ~4 million ([MarketBeat citing Compass CEO, May 2026](https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/compass-pathways-eyes-q4-fda-filing-for-psilocybin-depression-drug-after-phase-3-wins-2026-05-21/)).
- Lead asset: COMP360 (synthetic psilocybin). Two pivotal Phase 3 trials hit primary endpoint (COMP005 June 2025; COMP006 Feb 2026).
- Lead competitor (academic / non-profit): Usona Institute psilocybin for MDD.

### Major depressive disorder (MDD)
- Broader population than TRD.
- Usona Institute granted CNPV April 2026.

### Post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD)
- US adults with PTSD: ~13 million.
- MDMA-assisted therapy lead asset (Lykos / Resilient Pharmaceuticals) — CRL Aug 2024, additional Phase 3 required.
- Transcend Therapeutics' methylone (MDMA-analog, partnered with Otsuka) granted CNPV April 2026.
- VA opened internal MDMA-AT trial May 2026 ([VA News](https://news.va.gov/press-room/va-launches-mdma-assisted-mental-health-therapy-trial/)).

### Substance use disorders
- **Opioid use disorder / AUD:** ibogaine and noribogaine. DemeRx NB cleared IND April 2026 for Phase 1 of noribogaine in AUD — first FDA-authorized clinical study of an ibogaine derivative in the US ([Oria, 2026-05-06](https://oria.news/newsletters/psychedelic-therapy/issues/2026-05-06-psychedelics-could-be-fda-approved-this-summer/)).
- Texas SB 2308 (2025): $50M state funding for ibogaine clinical trials — largest US public investment in psychedelic research at the time.

### End-of-life distress in cancer
- Psilocybin: 1–2 doses with psychotherapy improve mood and existential distress (Jan 2025 study; [ScienceDaily](https://www.sciencedaily.com/news/mind_brain/psychedelic_drugs/)).

### Other active areas
- Generalized anxiety disorder (MindMed LSD)
- Postpartum depression (Cleveland Clinic Phase 1 synthetic non-hallucinogenic compound, April 2025)
- Traumatic brain injury (ibogaine; veterans data, observational)
- Eating disorders (early)
- Cluster headaches (psilocybin / DMT, early)

## Credibility hierarchy

- **High:** psilocybin → TRD/MDD (Phase 3 replicated).
- **Medium:** MDMA → PTSD (Phase 3 + CRL; rework underway).
- **Medium-low:** ibogaine → SUD/TBI (observational + Phase 1 only in US).
- **Preliminary:** all other indications.
