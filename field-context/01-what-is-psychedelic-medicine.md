# 01 · What is psychedelic medicine

**Phase:** 1 · **Scope:** in-scope · **Format:** Markdown with file paths

## What it is

Psychedelic medicine is the clinical use of serotonergic and related psychoactive compounds — psilocybin, MDMA, LSD, ibogaine, DMT, 5-MeO-DMT, ketamine — paired with structured psychotherapy, to treat mental health conditions. The model is **drug + therapy session + integration**, not a daily pill.

## Why it matters

- Targets conditions with high unmet need: treatment-resistant depression (TRD), PTSD, substance use disorders, end-of-life distress.
- Outcomes in trials are measured in weeks to months from 1–3 dosing sessions, not daily dosing — a different economic and clinical model than SSRIs.
- First classic-psychedelic FDA approval (Compass Pathways' COMP360 psilocybin) is a "genuine prospect" with NDA filing planned Q4 2026, per [Chambers practice guide May 2026](https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/medical-cannabis-psychedelic-medicines-2026).

## Key facts

- Most compounds remain **Schedule I** in the US federally as of May 2026 ([Recovered, 2026-05-19](https://recovered.org/blog/trump-eo-psychedelic-treatment-research-expansion)).
- **Ketamine** is the only legally prescribable option in the US today (Schedule III; off-label for depression; Spravato/esketamine FDA-approved for TRD).
- The April 18, 2026 White House Executive Order ("Accelerating Medical Treatments for Serious Mental Illness") created National Priority Vouchers, automatic rescheduling on Phase 3 completion, and a $50M ARPA-H matching grant for state programs ([Journey Clinical, May 2026](https://www.journeyclinical.com/resources/psychedelics-the-path-forward-what-the-2026-white-house-executive-order-means-for-clinicians-and-patients)).

## Current state (May 2026)

- COMP360 psilocybin: rolling NDA underway, Q4 2026 filing target ([Compass Q1 2026 release](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/compass-pathways-announces-first-quarter-103000764.html)).
- Lykos / Resilient Pharmaceuticals MDMA: Complete Response Letter Aug 2024; additional Phase 3 required; ~75% workforce reduction reported ([Chambers, May 2026](https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/medical-cannabis-psychedelic-medicines-2026)).
- VA launched MDMA-assisted therapy trial for PTSD + AUD in veterans, May 2026 ([VA News, 2026-05-27](https://news.va.gov/press-room/va-launches-mdma-assisted-mental-health-therapy-trial/)).

## Open questions

- Will the model survive at scale without trained therapists? Insurance coverage was explicitly excluded from the April 2026 EO.
- How is "set and setting" preserved in commercial clinics?
- Long-term durability past 12 months remains a primary FDA concern.
