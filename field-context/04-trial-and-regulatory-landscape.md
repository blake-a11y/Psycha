# 04 · Trial and regulatory landscape

**Phase:** 1 · **Scope:** in-scope · **Format:** Markdown with file paths

## Snapshot (as of May 2026)

- 65+ ongoing US psychedelic clinical trials as of 2024 ([SNS Insider report, May 2026](https://www.snsinsider.com/reports/psychedelic-drugs-market-7467)).
- First FDA approval of a classic psychedelic is a genuine prospect within 12 months.

## US: FDA

- **Breakthrough Therapy designation** held by MDMA, psilocybin (multiple sponsors), LSD.
- **Commissioner's National Priority Voucher (CNPV)** — created by April 18, 2026 EO. Can compress FDA review from 10–12 months to **1–2 months** after NDA submission.
- **First three CNPVs awarded April 24, 2026:**
  - Compass Pathways — COMP360 psilocybin / TRD
  - Usona Institute — psilocybin / MDD
  - Transcend Therapeutics — methylone / PTSD (partnered with Otsuka)
  - Source: [Chambers Practice Guide, May 2026](https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/medical-cannabis-psychedelic-medicines-2026)
- **DemeRx NB IND cleared April 2026** for Phase 1 noribogaine in AUD — first FDA-authorized ibogaine-derivative clinical study in the US.
- FDA Commissioner Marty Makary said in April 2026 the FDA expected to issue a decision by **late summer or fall 2026** for the most advanced candidate, administered in supervised clinical settings ([Medscape, May 15 2026](https://www.medscape.com/viewarticle/fda-psychiatry-roundup-april-2026-2026a1000fqf)).

## US: DEA

- All classic psychedelics (psilocybin, LSD, DMT, MDMA, ibogaine) remain **Schedule I** as of May 2026.
- Per the April 2026 EO, completing Phase 3 **automatically initiates** the rescheduling review — previously this required full FDA approval first.

## US: states

- **Texas:** SB 2308 (2025) — $50M for ibogaine trials. Clinical research at Baylor College of Medicine and UT Austin.
- **Utah:** HB 390 (2026) — state-funded clinical trial of psilocybin, MDMA, DMT for veterans with TRD-PTSD at University of Utah.
- **Kentucky, Mississippi:** authorized state-funded clinical trials for ibogaine.
- **Oregon, Colorado:** statewide regulated psilocybin services programs (non-medical model; outside FDA pathway).
- **Louisiana:** state House advanced a bill May 2026 to create a state-run psychedelic research initiative.

## Setbacks

- **Lykos Therapeutics MDMA-AT for PTSD:** FDA Complete Response Letter Aug 2024 (made public Sept 2025). Reasons: durability, safety reporting, bias concerns. Lykos now operating as **Resilient Pharmaceuticals** after ~75% workforce reduction ([Chambers, 2026](https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/medical-cannabis-psychedelic-medicines-2026)).

## Right to Try

- April 2026 EO opened Right-to-Try access for Phase 1-complete psychedelics to patients with severe or terminal illness — **only if a sponsor agrees to supply the drug**. No company has publicly committed as of May 2026.

## Non-US

- **Australia (2023):** first nation to permit prescribing of MDMA (for PTSD) and psilocybin (for TRD) by authorized psychiatrists.
- **UK:** Compass holds Innovative Licensing and Access Pathway (ILAP) designation for COMP360 in TRD.
- **Switzerland:** limited compassionate use of MDMA and LSD long-standing.
- **Canada:** Special Access Program for psilocybin in end-of-life distress.
