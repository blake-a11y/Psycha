# 05 · Key institutions and funders

**Phase:** 1 · **Scope:** in-scope · **Format:** Markdown with file paths

## Academic research centers

- **Johns Hopkins Center for Psychedelic and Consciousness Research** — founded 2019; Matthew Johnson (now departed), Roland Griffiths (deceased 2023) legacy program; psilocybin for depression, addiction, end-of-life distress.
- **Imperial College London — Centre for Psychedelic Research** — David Nutt, founding; original home of Robin Carhart-Harris.
- **UCSF — Translational Psychedelic Research (TrPR) Program / Carhart-Harris Lab** — current home of Robin Carhart-Harris (Ralph Metzner Distinguished Professor of Neurology).
- **NYU Langone Center for Psychedelic Medicine** — cancer distress, alcohol use disorder; Stephen Ross.
- **Yale Program for Psychedelic Science** — depression, suicidality; ketamine/psilocybin.
- **Stanford Exploratory Therapeutics Laboratory** — MDMA, psilocybin, neuroimaging.
- **Mount Sinai Center for Psychedelic Psychotherapy and Trauma Research** — Rachel Yehuda; veterans, trauma.
- **University of Utah** — Spencer Hyde / state-funded veteran psilocybin-MDMA-DMT trial.
- **Baylor College of Medicine and UT Austin** — Texas SB 2308 ibogaine programs.
- **University of Wisconsin–Madison** — Charles Raison (Usona principal investigator).

## Non-profit / advocacy funders

- **MAPS (Multidisciplinary Association for Psychedelic Studies)** — founded 1986 by Rick Doblin; original MDMA-PTSD program (transferred to Lykos/Resilient).
- **Usona Institute** — Madison-based non-profit; open-science psilocybin development.
- **Heffter Research Institute** — funds academic psilocybin research.
- **Beckley Foundation** — UK-based; Amanda Feilding; policy + research.

## Government / public funders

- **NIH / NIMH** — historically slow to fund psychedelics; growing as of 2024–2026.
- **DOD / VA** — VA opened first MDMA-AT trial May 2026; DOD funded MDMA-PTSD via prior agreements.
- **ARPA-H** — under the April 2026 EO, $50M matching grant for state psychedelic programs.
- **State legislatures** — Texas ($50M, 2025), Utah, Kentucky, Mississippi, Louisiana, Colorado, Oregon.

## Private capital

- Psychedelic drugs market: **$1.82B (2025) → projected $5.16B by 2034**, CAGR 12.27% ([Fortune Business Insights, May 2026](https://www.fortunebusinessinsights.com/psychedelic-drugs-market-109470)).
- Major PE / venture participation has been thin and burned in 2022–2024 (Compass and Atai down sharply from 2021 peaks). Late-2025 / 2026 EO has re-opened LP interest.
