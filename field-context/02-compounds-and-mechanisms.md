# 02 · Compounds and mechanisms

**Phase:** 1 · **Scope:** in-scope · **Format:** Markdown with file paths

## What it is

The clinical psychedelic class is heterogeneous. Mechanisms differ. Grouping them as one is convenient but wrong.

## Main compounds

| Compound | Class | Primary receptor mechanism | Lead indication in trials | US schedule (May 2026) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Psilocybin | Classic serotonergic | 5-HT2A agonist | Treatment-resistant depression, MDD | I |
| LSD | Classic serotonergic | 5-HT2A agonist | Generalized anxiety (MindMed) | I |
| DMT / 5-MeO-DMT | Classic serotonergic | 5-HT2A agonist (short-acting) | MDD, TRD | I |
| MDMA | Entactogen | Serotonin + dopamine release, oxytocin signaling | PTSD | I |
| Ibogaine / noribogaine | Atypical | NMDA antagonism, mu-opioid modulation, kappa | Opioid use disorder, AUD | I |
| Ketamine / esketamine | Dissociative | NMDA antagonist | TRD, suicidality | III (esketamine FDA-approved as Spravato) |
| Methylone | MDMA-analog | Monoamine releaser, less neurotoxic profile claimed | PTSD | I |

## Mechanistic working theories

- **Classic psychedelics (5-HT2A):** acutely increase **brain entropy** — diversity of neural activity — followed by lasting structural changes (denser DTI tracts up to 1 month post-dose; healthy volunteer data from Carhart-Harris lab, [Nature Communications via Medical Xpress, 2026-05-05](https://medicalxpress.com/news/2026-05-dose-psilocybin-human-brain-higher.html)).
- **MDMA:** reduces amygdala threat response, increases oxytocin, opens a therapeutic window for trauma processing — supports trauma reconsolidation in PTSD trials.
- **Ibogaine:** acts on multiple receptors; produces a long oneirogenic (dream-like) state. Cardiac QT prolongation is the dominant safety concern.
- **Ketamine:** NMDA blockade upregulates BDNF and AMPA signaling; rapid anti-depressant effect within hours; effect duration days to weeks per dose.

## Credibility levels

- Entropy / neural-plasticity mechanism: **peer-reviewed, replicated** (Carhart-Harris, Imperial → UCSF).
- "Ego dissolution" as therapeutic mediator: **peer-reviewed, contested**.
- "Microdosing" benefits: **mostly preliminary or anecdotal**; blinded trials largely negative.

## Open questions

- Is the subjective trip itself therapeutically necessary, or can non-hallucinogenic 5-HT2A agonists work? Active debate; companies on both sides.
- How much of the effect is set, setting, and therapist relationship vs the compound?
- Long-term effects on cardiac valves (5-HT2B agonism) under repeated dosing — flagged risk for chronic regimens.
