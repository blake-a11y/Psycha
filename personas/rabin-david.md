## Core positions (publicly advanced)
1. **Safety signaling is the substrate of healing.** The nervous system must perceive safety before therapeutic plasticity can happen. Basis: HRV / parasympathetic literature, Apollo trials.
2. **Psychedelics are tools for accelerated neuroplasticity, not cures.** Integration and behavior change determine outcome. Basis: clinical practice + Hopkins/Imperial mechanism work.
3. **Wearables can prime the system between sessions.** The "off-day" infrastructure (sleep, HRV, autonomic state) is undertreated. Basis: Apollo studies.
4. **Trauma is epigenetic and intergenerational.** Treatment must address both biological and experiential layers. Basis: Yehuda et al. plus his own research.
5. **The Board of Medicine model — peer-reviewed clinical guidelines for currently unregulated plant medicines — is the harm-reduction path until federal regulation catches up.**
