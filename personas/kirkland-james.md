## Primary research focus
- Cellular senescence biology — senescence-associated secretory phenotype (SASP), senescent-cell heterogeneity, tissue dysfunction with age.
- Senolytics — small molecules that selectively eliminate senescent cells. Lead compound class: dasatinib + quercetin (D+Q).
- Clinical translation of senolytics into age-related disease indications: diabetic kidney disease, idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis, frailty, osteoporosis, Alzheimer's.
- Mechanistic work on senescent-cell anti-apoptotic pathways (SCAPs).

## Core positions (3–6 theses they advance publicly)
1. Cellular senescence is a causal driver of age-related multi-morbidity, not just a marker. — basis: peer-reviewed mouse genetics and intervention data.
2. Senolytics can selectively eliminate senescent cells with "hit-and-run" intermittent dosing; short half-life drugs (D+Q) decrease senescent-cell burden in humans. — basis: Justice et al. *EBioMedicine* 2019, [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31542391/).
3. Senolytics are likely to deliver clinical value first in specific tissue-driven indications (kidney, lung, bone) rather than as general-purpose anti-aging drugs. — basis: trial-design argument.
4. Direct-to-consumer "senolytic" supplements (fisetin-based and others) outpace the evidence; clinical translation requires randomized data. — basis: stated public position.
5. Aging biology can be translated through standard FDA pathways using age-related disease indications without requiring an "aging" indication. — basis: pragmatic translational stance ([Mayo Clinic 2024 senolytics article](https://www.mayoclinic.org/medical-professionals/endocrinology/news/senescent-cells-promising-anti-aging-targets-for-health-span-extension-and-the-treatment-of-osteoporosis/mac-20431073)).

## Evidence base
- **Peer-reviewed / clinical:** Strong. Multiple senolytic trials in humans (open-label and small RCTs) in diabetic kidney disease, IPF, frailty.
- **Preliminary / observational:** Senolytic trial signals are positive but small-sample; durable outcome data still accruing. Mayo group's May 2026 *Aging Cell* paper on synthetic DNA aptamer senolytic targeting marks newer mechanism class.
- **Commercial / anecdotal:** Multiple disclosed advisory and licensing roles with senolytic-focused companies (Unity Biotechnology, Mayo Clinic spinouts).
