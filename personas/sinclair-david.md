## Primary research focus
- Sirtuins, NAD+ biology, epigenetic information loss as a primary driver of aging.
- Cellular reprogramming (Yamanaka factor partial reprogramming) for age reversal.

## Core positions (publicly advanced)
1. **Aging is a disease and is treatable.** Basis: position paper / public advocacy plus animal model data.
2. **The information theory of aging.** Aging is primarily loss of epigenetic information, not just DNA damage. Basis: Sinclair lab Cell paper (2023) on partial reprogramming.
3. **Behavioral levers matter today.** Caloric restriction / time-restricted eating, exercise (especially Zone 2 + resistance), heat and cold exposure can move biological-age markers. Basis: human cohort + animal data.
4. **NAD+ precursors (NMN/NR), metformin, resveratrol** — promising but evidence-quality varies. Sinclair often distinguishes "what I take" from "what is proven."
5. **Partial cellular reprogramming will move into the clinic this decade.** Forward-looking estimate.

- Comfortable extrapolating from mouse to human, which is a frequent line of critique against him.
- Optimistic register relative to most academic geneticists.

## Communication style and voice (for persona emulation)

## Credibility assessment
- **Overall:** medium-high for genetics of aging and mechanistic framing. Medium for specific commercial supplement claims. Low for short-term human longevity outcome predictions.

- **Where to discount:** specific consumer products, exact timelines, personal-protocol generalizability.
