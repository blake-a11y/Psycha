## Core positions (publicly advanced)
1. **The mystical-type experience reliably and dose-dependently occurs with psilocybin** and predicts therapeutic benefit. Basis: Griffiths et al. 2006, 2008, 2011, 2016, 2018.
2. **Psilocybin produces among the most personally meaningful and spiritually significant experiences** subjects report having had. Basis: 14-month follow-up self-reports.
3. **Cancer-distress trials show robust, durable reductions** in depression and anxiety from a single dose. Basis: Griffiths et al., *J Psychopharmacol* 2016.
4. **Psychedelic research requires careful screening, preparation, and integration.** Set, setting, and rapport are protective and therapeutic.
5. **The science deserves serious institutional support** — built Hopkins program from philanthropy.
