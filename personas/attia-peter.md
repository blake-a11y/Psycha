## Core positions (3–6 theses they advance publicly)
1. Cardiovascular disease, cancer, neurodegeneration, and metabolic disease ("the Four Horsemen") drive most premature mortality and should be treated decades earlier than current US standard of care. — basis: epidemiology, mainstream cardiology; framing is his own.
2. ApoB, not LDL-C, is the operative metric for atherosclerotic risk; aggressive lipid lowering should start much earlier than guideline-driven care. — basis: peer-reviewed cardiology, contested by guideline bodies on timing.
3. VO2 max and muscle mass / strength in the top decile-for-age are the highest-leverage modifiable inputs for all-cause mortality. — basis: large observational cohorts; causal claims are inferential.
4. Healthspan and the "Marginal Decade" matter more than maximum lifespan; training should be back-cast from desired physical capacity at age 85+. — basis: framework argument.
5. Most pharmacologic longevity claims (rapamycin, metformin in non-diabetics, NAD precursors) are unproven for healthy people; use cautiously and label as preliminary. — basis: trial data, stated repeatedly on podcast.
