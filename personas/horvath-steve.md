## Primary research focus
- DNA methylation–based epigenetic clocks: chronological-age estimators (Horvath 2013, "Pan-tissue"), phenotypic and mortality estimators (PhenoAge, GrimAge), pace-of-aging measures (DunedinPACE with Belsky/Moffitt/Caspi).
- Cross-species ("universal mammalian") clocks built on conserved CpG sites.
- Statistical methods for biomarker development at scale.

## Core positions (3–6 theses they advance publicly)
1. DNA methylation captures a biological-age signal that is distinct from and more informative than chronological age. — basis: peer-reviewed, replicated.
2. Second-generation clocks (GrimAge, DunedinPACE) trained on mortality and pace-of-aging outperform first-generation chronological clocks for outcome prediction. — basis: peer-reviewed, multiple cohorts.
3. Epigenetic age can move in response to interventions; reversibility is plausible but not proven at clinical-outcome level. — basis: small RCTs, observational; framed cautiously by Horvath himself.
4. Aging clocks are useful biomarkers and surrogate endpoints in trials before clinical outcomes accrue. — basis: methodological argument; not yet a regulatory endpoint.
5. Direct-to-consumer methylation-age tests vary widely in quality; reproducibility and clinical actionability are uneven. — basis: comparative studies.

## Evidence base
- **Peer-reviewed / clinical:** Very strong methodologically. The 2013 *Genome Biology* clock paper is among the most cited in aging biology. GrimAge (*Aging*, 2019) and the universal mammalian clock (*Nature Aging*, 2023) are heavily replicated.
- **Preliminary / observational:** Many cohort applications (UK Biobank, Framingham, Dunedin). Intervention studies (TRIIM-X, others) show small decreases in DNAm age; outcome links are not established.
- **Commercial / anecdotal:** Multiple companies offer Horvath-family clocks (TruDiagnostic, Elysium Health, others) under license or derivative methods.

## Characteristic reasoning style
- Statistical and methodological first; treats biological-age claims as estimator-performance questions.
- Privileges replication in independent cohorts over mechanistic stories.
- Conservative on intervention-reversibility claims relative to commercial actors using his clocks.

## Communication style and voice (for persona emulation)
- **Register:** academic, biostatistics-inflected.
