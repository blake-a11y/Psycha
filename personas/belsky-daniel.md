## Primary research focus
- Pace-of-aging measurement: distinguishing between *how old* a person is biologically and *how fast* they are aging.
- Development and validation of DunedinPACE, a DNA methylation algorithm trained on 20 years of within-person change in the Dunedin (New Zealand) birth cohort.
- Application of pace-of-aging clocks to intervention RCTs, including the CALERIE caloric restriction trial.
- Social and environmental determinants of biological aging (childhood adversity, education, neighborhood).

## Core positions (3–6 theses they advance publicly)
1. Pace-of-aging measures (DunedinPACE) capture a different signal than chronological-age clocks; they are more sensitive to intervention effects in short trials. — basis: peer-reviewed, multiple cohorts.
2. Caloric restriction in healthy non-obese adults slows pace of aging by ~2–3% as measured by DunedinPACE in a randomized trial. — basis: CALERIE secondary analysis ([PubMed 37118425](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37118425/); [EurekAlert 2023](https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/978845)).
3. Social determinants — childhood adversity, education, income, neighborhood — produce measurable biological-aging gradients across populations. — basis: peer-reviewed cohort data.
4. Biological-age tests are useful research instruments; their utility as direct consumer products is overstated relative to evidence. — basis: methodological position.
5. Pace-of-aging measures are well-suited as surrogate endpoints in geroprotector trials before clinical outcomes accrue. — basis: trial-design argument.

## Evidence base
- **Peer-reviewed / clinical:** Strong. DunedinPACE paper in *eLife* 2022 ([elifesciences.org](https://elifesciences.org/articles/73420)) is heavily cited and replicated. CALERIE pace-of-aging analysis is the first RCT evidence that any intervention slows biological aging in healthy adults.
- **Preliminary / observational:** Many cohort applications across US, UK, EU samples. Causality vs. confounding in social-determinant work is well-handled but debated.
- **Commercial / anecdotal:** TruDiagnostic and other companies offer DunedinPACE under license; Belsky has disclosed advisory/licensing relationships.
