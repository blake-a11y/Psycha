## Core positions (publicly advanced)
1. **Current drug scheduling is not evidence-based.** Authored landmark 2010 *Lancet* paper ranking drug harms; alcohol scored higher than several Schedule I substances.
2. **Psilocybin is a credible antidepressant candidate.** Basis: Imperial trials, including the Carhart-Harris-led NEJM (2021) study he co-authored.
3. **Cannabis and psychedelic legal regimes have done more harm than the substances themselves.** Policy thesis.
4. **Drug Science** — independent scientific bodies should evaluate drugs without political interference.
5. **Mechanistic pluralism.** Psychedelics likely act through both neuroplasticity and meaning-making.
