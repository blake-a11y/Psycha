## Primary research focus
- Psilocybin for major depressive disorder.

## Core positions (publicly advanced)
1. **Single-dose psilocybin produces rapid and durable antidepressant effects in MDD.** Basis: Raison CL et al., *JAMA* 2023 (n=104, 6-week placebo-controlled).
2. **Conscious awareness of the trip is likely necessary for durable benefit.** Active investigation via CoPE / RECAP studies (designed to deliver psilocybin to asleep or amnestic patients).
3. **Depression has an inflammatory substrate** in many patients; psychedelics may interact with it.
4. **Open-science non-profit development is a legitimate alternative to venture-backed psychedelic biotech.** Basis: Usona's structural model.
5. **Compassion training has measurable biological effects** — adjacent to but separable from psychedelic work.
