## Core positions (3–6 theses they advance publicly)
1. Aging is fundamentally a metabolic process; metabolic interventions (fasting, caloric restriction, ketogenic states) act on conserved pathways that also extend lifespan in model organisms. — basis: peer-reviewed, multiple species.
2. β-hydroxybutyrate functions as an endogenous HDAC inhibitor and signaling molecule, not just an energy substrate. — basis: peer-reviewed (Shimazu/Verdin *Science* 2013; "Ketone bodies as signaling metabolites" review, *Trends in Endocrinology & Metabolism* 2014, [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24140022/)).
3. Sirtuins and HDACs link nutrient sensing to gene expression and chromatin state across tissues. — basis: peer-reviewed.
4. The Buck Institute's role is to drive translational pipelines for the geroscience hypothesis, including senolytics, partial reprogramming, and immune rejuvenation. — basis: institute strategic positioning.
5. Consumer longevity products are running ahead of evidence; the field needs disciplined translational science, not supplement marketing. — basis: public statements.
