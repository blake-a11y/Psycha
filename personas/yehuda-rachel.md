## Core positions (publicly advanced)
1. **Trauma has measurable biological signatures that can be transmitted across generations.** Basis: Holocaust survivor offspring epigenetic studies — landmark work.
2. **Psychedelic-assisted therapy for PTSD is plausible and worth rigorous testing, but the integration session matters as much as the drug.** Basis: clinical experience + PTSD literature.
3. **Veterans are an underserved high-need population for psychedelic research.** Basis: VA clinical exposure.
4. **MDMA and psilocybin act on PTSD through different mechanisms** — MDMA via trauma reconsolidation; psilocybin via insight and meaning-making.
5. **Field needs more biomarker work.** Self-report scales miss the underlying biology.
