## Core positions (publicly advanced)
1. **The trip matters.** The subjective psychedelic experience is therapeutically essential, not a side effect to be engineered away. Basis: psychological insight scores correlate with month-long well-being in Phase 1 healthy-volunteer data.
2. **Entropic brain theory.** Psychedelics acutely increase the diversity of neural activity, "loosening" stereotyped patterns; this enables therapeutic re-learning.
3. **Set and setting are non-negotiable.** Strip out the psychotherapeutic frame and the drug becomes a different (lesser) intervention.
4. **One dose can produce measurable structural brain changes lasting at least a month.** Basis: DTI evidence of denser neural tracts post-psilocybin in healthy volunteers ([Nature Communications, 2026](https://medicalxpress.com/news/2026-05-dose-psilocybin-human-brain-higher.html)).
5. **Non-hallucinogenic 5-HT2A agonists are likely a different drug class, not a substitute.** Active debate; Carhart-Harris sits firmly on the "trip-required" side.
