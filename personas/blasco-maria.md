## Primary research focus
- Telomere biology and telomerase as mechanisms of cellular aging and cancer.
- Mouse models of telomerase gain-of-function and loss-of-function.
- Telomerase gene therapy (AAV-TERT) in adult mice — demonstrated lifespan extension without cancer increase.
- Telomere syndromes (dyskeratosis congenita, idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis, aplastic anemia) as natural disease models for telomere-driven aging.
- Interplay of telomeres with stem-cell biology and induced pluripotency.

## Core positions (3–6 theses they advance publicly)
1. Telomere shortening is a primary, causal hallmark of aging — not just a correlate. — basis: mouse loss-of-function and gain-of-function genetics, peer-reviewed.
2. Telomerase reactivation in adult tissues can extend lifespan in mice without increasing cancer incidence when delivered transiently via AAV. — basis: Blasco lab *EMBO Mol Med* 2012 and follow-on work.
3. Short telomeres define a treatable disease class (telomere syndromes) and may identify subgroups of common diseases (pulmonary fibrosis, aplastic anemia) responsive to telomere-targeted therapy. — basis: human genetics + mouse models.
4. Targeting telomeres negatively in cancer ("removing immortality") is a parallel therapeutic strategy; pro-telomerase for aging, anti-telomerase for tumors. — basis: CNIO group work, including 2023 *Cell Death & Differentiation* paper on NSCLC ([CNIO 2023](https://www.cnio.es/en/news/cnio-researchers-show-that-targeting-telomeres-might-be-an-effective-therapeutic-strategy-against-lung-cancer/)).
5. The hallmarks framework remains the best organizing structure for the field. — basis: co-author on 2013 and 2023 papers.

## Evidence base
- **Peer-reviewed / clinical:** Strong basic science. Telomerase gene therapy lifespan extension demonstrated repeatedly in mice. No completed human telomerase therapy trials for aging.
- **Preliminary / observational:** Human telomere-length / leukocyte-telomere associations with disease are well-documented but causality contested.
- **Commercial / anecdotal:** Limited direct commercial role. Telomerase-activation supplements (TA-65 family) are commercial but not Blasco-affiliated; she has publicly maintained academic distance from supplement marketing.

- Treats telomere length as a mechanism, not a consumer biomarker.

## Known biases, financial interests, or blind spots
- Career-long advocacy for telomere causality; framing aligned with that hypothesis.
- CNIO institutional interest in maintaining telomere program funding.

## Credibility assessment
