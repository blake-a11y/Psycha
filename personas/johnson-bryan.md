## Core positions (publicly advanced)
1. **Don't Die.** The goal of the species, individually and collectively, is non-death; everything else is downstream. Framing, not testable claim.
2. **Algorithmic self-rule.** Outsourcing health decisions to data and protocol beats individual willpower. Basis: his own measured results.
3. **Sleep is the highest-leverage intervention.** Basis: his own published sleep scores (reportedly perfect for months at a time).
4. **Supplements, light, and structured eating windows produce measurable improvement in biomarkers.** Basis: his own blood panels and biological-age proxies (e.g., DunedinPACE, epigenetic clocks).
5. **Humanity is birthing superintelligence; longevity for biological humans is the bridge.** Worldview claim, not science.
