# 03 · Intervention landscape and evidence tiers

**Phase:** 1 · **Scope:** in-scope · **Format:** Markdown with file paths

## What it is

A map of every major intervention class in longevity, sorted by **evidence tier**, not by marketing claim. The split matters because the field's defining failure mode is selling `[commercial]`-tier interventions as if they were `[validated clinical]`.

## Evidence-tier ladder (used throughout)

| Tier | Definition |
|---|---|
| `[validated clinical]` | Phase 3 / FDA approval / large-cohort longitudinal data. Mechanism + outcome both confirmed. |
| `[preliminary clinical]` | Phase 1/2, small RCTs, single-site, or strong observational data. Directionally suggestive. |
| `[commercial / supplement]` | Sold as longevity product. Human evidence thin or surrogate-only. Marketing exceeds data. |
| `[self-experimenter / anecdotal]` | n=1 protocols, podcaster routines, public-figure stacks. Not evidence. |

## Tier 1 — Validated clinical (for the underlying mechanism or chronic disease — **not** for "anti-aging")

These are the foundations a serious longevity protocol rests on. None are FDA-approved for aging. All are validated for the disease they were developed against.

- **Statins** — cardiovascular mortality reduction. Decades of Phase 3. `[validated clinical]`
- **GLP-1 agonists** (semaglutide, tirzepatide) — diabetes, weight, CV outcomes (SELECT trial). Increasing evidence in kidney, heart-failure, MASH/MASH. **Not approved for aging.** `[validated clinical]` for labeled indications.
- **Resistance + aerobic exercise** — all-cause mortality, sarcopenia prevention, cognitive function. The single best-supported longevity intervention. `[validated clinical]`
- **Sleep optimization (7–9 hr)** — observational data robust; mechanistic links to amyloid clearance, glucose regulation. `[validated clinical]` as a risk factor; `[preliminary]` as an "intervention" with quantified gain.
- **Standard preventive cardiology + cancer screening** — colonoscopy, mammography, ApoB, Lp(a), coronary calcium. `[validated clinical]`

### Off-label longevity use (validated for one thing, used for another)

- **Metformin** — Type 2 diabetes drug. Off-label longevity use is widespread. **Off-label use is legal; longevity benefit is not established in healthy adults.** The TAME trial is the active attempt to test this. See file 04. `[preliminary clinical]` for off-label longevity claim.
- **Rapamycin (sirolimus)** — Approved for organ transplant immunosuppression and certain cancers. Off-label intermittent low-dose use for longevity is common in clinics. PEARL is the largest healthy-adult trial: primary endpoint failed; safety acceptable; secondary signals small and underpowered ([PEARL review, May 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=THhlMHJJWs8)). `[preliminary clinical]` for longevity.
- **SGLT2 inhibitors** — Diabetes, heart failure, CKD. Mechanistic appeal for aging. No aging-specific human data. `[preliminary clinical]` for off-label longevity.

## Tier 2 — Preliminary clinical (active human trials; outcomes uncertain)

- **Senolytics**
  - D+Q (dasatinib + quercetin): Phase 2 in IPF, diabetic kidney disease, Alzheimer's. Mayo Clinic 2019 pilot is the proof-of-concept.
  - UBX1325 (foselutoclax, Unity Biotechnology): Phase 2b ASPIRE complete; BEHOLD in *NEJM Evidence* April 2025. Diabetic macular edema indication.
  - CAR-T senolytics (uPAR-targeting) — Phase 1, Cold Spring Harbor / Stanford spinouts.
  - ~30+ active trials industry-wide as of mid-2026 ([Senolytic Therapy Market Report](https://marketintelo.com/report/senolytic-therapy-market)).
  - Tier: `[preliminary clinical]`.
- **Partial epigenetic reprogramming** — Life Biosciences ER-100 Phase 1 cleared 2026 for optic-nerve disease (NAION, glaucoma). NewLimit, Retro Biosciences, Altos Labs are preclinical-to-IND. `[preliminary clinical]`.
- **Mitochondrial augmentation** — Minovia Therapeutics ($180M Series D, 2025); cell-based mitochondrial transfer in pediatric mitochondrial disease and age-related decline. `[preliminary clinical]`.
- **Plasma exchange / dilution** — Circulate Health ($12M, 2025) operating commercial TPE. Mechanistic basis: Conboy lab parabiosis. Human longevity-endpoint data not available. `[preliminary clinical / commercial]` hybrid — sold today as a service while data accumulates.
- **GLP-1 agonists for non-diabetic aging endpoints** — cardiovascular event reduction in non-diabetics (SELECT) is `[validated]`; dementia and frailty endpoints still in trial. `[preliminary clinical]` for the longevity framing.
- **Canine aging (Loyal)** — $100M Series C Feb 2026. FDA pathway via Center for Veterinary Medicine. Provides regulatory template for "aging as a treatable condition" in a species before human attempts. `[preliminary clinical]` for the molecule; `[validated]` for the regulatory pathway as proof-of-concept.

## Tier 3 — Commercial / supplement

Sold as longevity products. Human evidence is thin, surrogate-only, or absent for the marketed benefit.

- **NAD+ precursors (NMN, NR)** — raise blood NAD+ in humans; tissue NAD+ rise and functional benefit in healthy adults not established. ConsumerLab review (May 2026) and NPR coverage agree the marketing exceeds the data ([ConsumerLab](https://www.consumerlab.com/reviews/nmn-nadh-nicotinamide-riboside/nmn-nadh-nicotinamide-riboside/); [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/05/11/nx-s1-5813664/nad-infusions-supplements-longevity-science)). `[commercial / supplement]`.
- **IV NAD+ infusions** — Pojednic lab notes inefficient cellular uptake; abdominal cramping, nausea common adverse events. `[commercial / anecdotal]`.
- **Resveratrol, pterostilbene, fisetin** — robust in vitro and rodent data; thin human data for healthspan claims. Fisetin Phase 2 trials in progress at Mayo. `[commercial / supplement]` today; `[preliminary clinical]` where trials are running.
- **Spermidine, urolithin A, glycine + NAC (GlyNAC)** — small human trials with biomarker improvements; no Phase 3. `[commercial / supplement]`.
- **Peptides** (BPC-157, TB-500, epitalon, etc.) — sold by many clinics. **Not FDA-approved** for any longevity indication. FDA has restricted compounding of several. `[commercial / anecdotal]`.
- **Hormone "optimization"** (TRT in low-normal men, DHEA, HGH peptides like CJC/Ipamorelin) — TRT in clinical hypogonadism is `[validated]`; "optimization" in normal-range patients is `[commercial / anecdotal]`. GH peptide use for longevity is `[commercial / anecdotal]`.
- **Stem-cell therapy** (mesenchymal, exosome, "regenerative") — sold widely by clinics. Very few are FDA-cleared for the indications marketed. `[commercial / anecdotal]` for the longevity claim.

## Tier 4 — Self-experimenter / anecdotal

Public protocols. Compelling biomarker logs. Not evidence for the general population.

- **Project Blueprint (Bryan Johnson)** — ~100+ supplement and lifestyle interventions, daily measurement, public dataset. n=1. Multiple measured biological-age clocks moved favorably over multiple years per his public reports. Not a controlled trial. `[self-experimenter / anecdotal]`.
- **Attia-style "Medicine 3.0"** — ApoB-aggressive, Zone 2 + VO2max, strength, sleep, mental health. The framework rests on `[validated]` and `[preliminary]` building blocks; the bundle as a "longevity protocol" is `[anecdotal]` because the bundle has never been tested as a unit.
- **Huberman / podcast stacks** — supplement and lifestyle bundles communicated via podcast. Heterogeneous, often citing animal data for human conclusions. `[anecdotal]`.

## Mapping interventions to hallmarks (quick reference)

| Hallmark | Most-tested intervention | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| Genomic instability | DNA-repair-supporting (NAD+, sirtuin activators) | `[preliminary / commercial]` |
| Telomere attrition | TA-65, telomerase activators | `[commercial]` |
| Epigenetic alterations | Partial reprogramming (Life Bio, NewLimit, Altos) | `[preliminary clinical]` |
| Loss of proteostasis | Autophagy inducers (spermidine, rapamycin) | `[preliminary / commercial]` |
| Disabled macroautophagy | Rapamycin, fasting | `[preliminary clinical]` |
| Deregulated nutrient sensing | Metformin, rapamycin, GLP-1, CR | `[preliminary]` / off-label `[validated]` |
| Mitochondrial dysfunction | Urolithin A, mitochondrial transfer (Minovia) | `[preliminary / commercial]` |
| Cellular senescence | D+Q, UBX1325, navitoclax, fisetin | `[preliminary clinical]` |
| Stem cell exhaustion | HSC rejuvenation (HexemBio), Yamanaka reprogramming | `[preliminary clinical]` |
| Altered intercellular communication | Plasma exchange / dilution | `[preliminary / commercial]` |
| Chronic inflammation | Statins, GLP-1, IL-6 inhibitors, lifestyle | off-label `[validated]` |
| Dysbiosis | Targeted probiotics, dietary fiber, FMT | `[preliminary]` |

## Open questions

- Will any Tier 3 intervention move to Tier 1 over the next 5 years? Candidates: senolytics (D+Q in IPF/CKD), NAD+ precursors (kidney, peripheral artery), GLP-1 in non-diabetic aging endpoints.
- Does combining interventions amplify benefit, or compete for the same downstream surrogate (e.g. methylation clock movement)?
- Is there a defensible model where a clinic can deliver only `[validated]` + `[preliminary]` tier interventions and survive economically? Most clinics today depend on Tier 3 / Tier 4 revenue.
