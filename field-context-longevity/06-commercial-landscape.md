# 06 · Commercial landscape

**Phase:** 1 · **Scope:** in-scope · **Format:** Markdown with file paths

## What it is

The companies and operating models monetizing longevity today. Five categories:

1. **Diagnostics** — biological-age tests, preventive imaging, preventive lab panels.
2. **Clinics** — physician-led longevity / preventive care delivery.
3. **Consumer brands** — supplements, food, programs.
4. **Software / wearables** — measurement, AI health intelligence, behavior.
5. **Drug developers** — therapeutics targeting aging mechanisms via disease indications.

Funding is concentrated. YTD 2026: ~$233.9M across 5 disclosed pure-play deals; **81.4% to therapeutics developers**; top 3 deals = 92.6% of capital ([New Market Pitch, May 2026](https://newmarketpitch.com/blogs/news/longevity-funding-trends)). Maturing at the top, experimental in the long tail.

## Why it matters

- This is where investor checks land and where consumer dollars get spent.
- The credibility gradient is severe: drug developers and large preventive platforms have institutional capital; clinics and consumer brands depend on cash-pay revenue and influencer marketing.
- An operator or PE buyer must read each category by its evidence tier and regulatory exposure separately.

## 1. Diagnostics

### Biological-age tests
- **Epigenetic clocks:** Horvath (2013), GrimAge, DunedinPACE, PhenoAge, OMICmAge. Sold direct-to-consumer and through clinics.
- **DTC operators:** TruDiagnostic (DunedinPACE-licensed), Elysium Index, MyDNAge, GlycanAge (glycan-based, not methylation), TallyHealth, Generation Lab.
- **NAD diagnostics:** NADMED ($3.8M Series A, 2024) ([New Market Pitch](https://newmarketpitch.com/blogs/news/longevity-funding-trends)).
- Tier: `[preliminary clinical]` for analytical validity of well-trained clocks; `[anecdotal]` for the clinical-decision utility of moving a score.

### Preventive imaging
- Whole-body MRI (Prenuvo, Ezra), DEXA, CT coronary calcium, advanced cardiac imaging. Mostly cash-pay.
- Strongest validated longevity contribution: early disease detection, not biological-age reversal.

### Comprehensive lab platforms
- **Function Health** — $298M Series B, Nov 2025, led by Redpoint, a16z, Battery, NFDG ([New Market Pitch](https://newmarketpitch.com/blogs/news/longevity-funding-trends)). 100+ biomarkers per panel + AI health intelligence. Largest single longevity-adjacent consumer round of recent cycles.
- **Everlab** ($10M Seed, 2025, Left Lane Capital, APAC). AI-powered preventive diagnostics.
- **InsideTracker, Wild Health, Marek Health** — adjacent platforms.

## 2. Clinics

- Estimated **~800 US longevity clinics** operating ([Star-Telegram, May 2026](https://www.star-telegram.com/entertainment/living/article315797385.html)). `[validated]` for count; `[commercial]` for therapeutic claims most clinics make.

### Notable named clinics
- **Fountain Life** — AI-enabled preventive longevity clinics. $18M Series B, Aug 2025. Pricing from ~$20K/yr. ([Star-Telegram, May 2026](https://www.star-telegram.com/entertainment/living/article315797385.html)).
- **Human Longevity, Inc.** (San Diego / SF) — $39.8M Series B, Aug 2024 (TVM Capital Healthcare, Panacea Venture). Top-tier $19K/yr membership; precision medicine focus. Former CEO/President David Karow, public commentary emphasizes early-detection.
- **Next Health** — high-end concierge.
- **Restor Longevity Clinic** — cellular regeneration framing; pricing $4,000–$12,000 per program.
- **Synergy Life Centers** — accessible-tier ($1,500–$5,000 programs).
- **Humanaut Health** ($8.7M Seed, May 2024) — clinic platform.
- **Blue Longevity Clinic** ($2.3M, Jul 2025) — Europe.
- **TMRW** ($7M Seed, Mar 2026) — APAC, membership + clinic + epigenetic testing.

### Revenue mechanics
- Membership-tier from $1,500/yr to >$150,000/yr.
- Initial diagnostic assessments $500–$1,200.
- Treatment add-ons (peptides, hormone optimization, IV NAD+, stem-cell, hyperbaric, plasma therapies) are the higher-margin revenue. Most are `[commercial / anecdotal]` tier.
- Insurance: typically not covered.

### Risk profile
- Marketing-claim exposure (FDA, FTC).
- Peptide-compounding exposure (FDA restrictions 2024–2026).
- Off-label drug prescribing exposure (state medical boards).
- Lack of randomized evidence that clinic protocols extend lifespan is the central credibility weakness ([Star-Telegram, May 2026](https://www.star-telegram.com/entertainment/living/article315797385.html)).

## 3. Consumer brands

### Supplement-led brands
- **Bryan Johnson's "Don't Die" / Blueprint** — $60M financing in 2025; supplement + food + protocol brand ([New Market Pitch](https://newmarketpitch.com/blogs/news/longevity-funding-trends)). Defining example of self-experimenter → commercial brand.
- **L-Nutra** — $36.5M, Jan 2026, Series D+, Mubadala Investment Company. Fasting-mimicking-diet medical nutrition (ProLon brand) ([New Market Pitch](https://newmarketpitch.com/blogs/news/longevity-funding-trends)).
- **Elysium Health** — NR-based supplements; Charles Brenner / MIT-linked science board.
- **Tru Niagen / ChromaDex** — NR supplements; ConsumerLab independent review notes evidence limits.
- **SRW Laboratories** — research-led nutraceutical, NZ-origin.
- **Decode Age** ($1.7M, Dec 2025, APAC) — biomarker-linked supplement brand.
- **Qualia / Neurohacker Collective** — multi-ingredient stacks, including a senolytic-marketed product.
- **Renue, Thorne, Pure Encapsulations** — physician-channel longevity SKUs.

### Food / nutrition platforms
- **L-Nutra / ProLon** (above).
- **Levels, Nutrisense** — CGM-driven food platforms with longevity adjacency.

Tier: most products are `[commercial / supplement]`. A subset (NR/NMN, urolithin A, spermidine) has `[preliminary clinical]` data for narrower endpoints than the marketing implies.

## 4. Software, wearables, AI

- **WHOOP, Oura, Apple Watch, Garmin** — sleep, HRV, fitness. Validated for the underlying sensor measurements; longevity-impact claim is `[preliminary]` at best.
- **CGMs (Dexcom, Abbott, Levels-wrapped)** — glucose variability tracking.
- **PreemptiveAI** ($6.4M Seed, 2024) — biomedical foundation model on phone / wearable signals.
- **AAA LifeOS** ($5.6M Seed, Nov 2025) — AI longevity / digital continuity platform.
- **Aging Research Platforms (Insilico Medicine, $110M Series E 2025)** — AI drug discovery focused on age-related disease ([New Market Pitch](https://newmarketpitch.com/blogs/news/longevity-funding-trends)).

## What this map tells you (PE / operator)

- Pure-play longevity therapeutics is a high-conviction, large-check VC game ($50M–$200M Series A/B). Not a $5–10M PE lane.
- Clinics, supplements, and diagnostics are the $5–10M-check categories — but each carries commercial-claim and regulatory exposure.
- The most defensible PE thesis sits in **picks-and-shovels**: lab/diagnostic infrastructure, preventive lab platforms with longitudinal data moats, training/certification for clinicians, and tech that makes existing `[validated]`-tier interventions easier to deliver.
- The next 18 months' inflection events are: TAME enrollment status, first senolytic regulatory readout in a non-aging disease, partial-reprogramming Phase 1 safety read (ER-100), and any FDA action on the LDT rule.

## Open questions

- Will any clinic platform consolidate to a national, payer-aligned brand? (Today: Fountain Life, Human Longevity, Restor each operate at scale; none dominate.)
- Will a Tier-3 consumer-brand IPO succeed in 2027–2028 (Blueprint, Function Health–exit, Elysium)?
- Will FTC or state AG actions reset what longevity marketing claims are sayable?
